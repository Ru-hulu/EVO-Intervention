Container-safe overlap admission
NVML returns host PIDs that are not the PIDs in this container's /proc. Never
compare those numbers to local process ancestry, match them by GPU memory size,
or treat an inaccessible PID as an owned task. The dispatcher now uses an
explicit group-ownership proof: after the extra workers finish, both GPUs in a
training pair must each have exactly one distinct compute PID, continuously
observed at intervals <=30 seconds. The recorded torchrun must retain its exact
two configured ranks (including process start times), CUDA rank assignments,
and mapped NVIDIA device minors. After at least 60 seconds, a NEW training log
entry must prove that both ranks completed the native CUDA collective during
that interval. Only then are those singleton contexts attributed to that
training group. This is a temporal ownership proof, not a host/local PID map.
The latest collective must remain fresh (<=600 seconds); changed ranks, vanished
contexts or phase changes invalidate the proof. Extra/unknown PIDs on a candidate
GPU still prevent admission. Proofs are in dispatcher-status.gpu_ownership and
are rebuilt after scheduler reload. No running training code, driver, PID
namespace, or scientific configuration is changed. First-model real-episode
canaries and all existing resource/staggering guards remain mandatory.
